Use case 06

Engineering Recruiting & Ways of Working Matching

From a work-package requirement to an evidence-based, human-controlled hiring decision, showing the impact each candidate has on skills and on how the team works.

  • Work packageWP-EL-042
  • ProjectConverter Delivery · AC Yard
  • RoleProtection & Control Commissioning Engineer
  • Demand2 FTE within 8 weeks

01

Define the engineering requirement

The Engineering Manager creates the job straight from the work package, so recruiting starts from real project demand, not from a generic job ad.

Engineering Manager

  • Project, work package, role, engagement type and required availability in one form
  • Skills split into general professional and task-related competencies, each required or preferred
  • Software & test equipment from the installed project tooling (OMICRON, relay OEM tools, IEC 61850)
  • Team context with current → target Ways of Working

02

From gap to decision in six steps

Each step has a clear owner. Evidence is collected along the way, and the final decision always stays with a person.

Define the requirement

Engineering Manager

  • Work-package role, responsibilities and essential competencies
  • Evidence expected from every candidate, captured up front
  • The team's current vs. target Ways of Working across six dimensions
Full size

Identify the capability & WoW gap

Engineering Manager + Recruiter

  • Work-package demand compared against verified internal capacity
  • Result: a 2 FTE protection engineering gap with an 8-week deadline
  • Ways of Working are scored for the team, never for individuals
Full size

All candidates for the role at a glance

Recruiter · Engineering Manager

  • Switch between Overall, WoW and Skills Gap impact views
  • Each card shows the FTE it covers and the WoW changes, e.g. +6 Efficiency / −2 Employer Satisfaction
  • Gauges make positive and negative overall impact visible at once
Full size

Assess evidence & expected contribution

Candidate Evaluator + Engineering Manager

  • Every work-package requirement checked against the candidate's evidence
  • Expected contribution to the team's Ways of Working, written as a hypothesis
  • Evaluator comments and open questions recorded for the decision
Full size

Job vs. candidate: +14% overall impact

Engineering Manager

  • Overall impact = 50% skills gap closure (+15%) + 50% WoW gap closure (+13%)
  • 10 of 12 job requirements covered; the 2 gaps are flagged clearly
  • Expected WoW change per dimension against the team's targets
Full size

Decision impact & approval

HR Director

  • Decision impact: Agility +16 pp and Efficiency +14 pp, both targets reached
  • Business impact: 28% capability coverage (120 / 430 FTE) and 4 weeks to readiness; cost and quality still to validate
  • Decision approval: HR Expert → HR Director → Operations Lead & Finance Partner → Programme Sponsor

Candidate matching & normalized impact

Every candidate shows the impact they would have on skills coverage and on how the team works, normalized so candidates can be compared fairly.

Discover qualified specialists

Recruiter

  • One search across the internal workforce, the external talent pool and contract specialists
  • Anonymized candidate IDs with evidence status and availability
  • A match score alone is not enough: evidence comes before the shortlist

Impact measured where it matters

Recruiter · Engineering Manager

  • Choose the scope: organisation-wide, department, project or work package
  • The same candidates are recalculated against the chosen scope

Compare & shortlist

Recruiter

  • The same objective criteria for every candidate: evidence, availability, WoW contribution
  • A clear recommendation: who goes to review, who needs more evidence
  • Privacy & fairness: no inferred diversity or personality scoring

  • Experience & availability kept separate from generic CV matching

04

Decision impact

Move 120 specialists. Close critical gaps. Converter Delivery, 12-month programme, 430 FTE capability gap.

Workforce decision: Agility

HR Director

  • Redeploy 120 internal specialists: protect critical engineering delivery while closing capability gaps
  • 120 FTE proposed redeployment, 430 FTE capability gap, 28% potential coverage, $2.1M programme opportunity
  • Agility selected: 58 → 74, target 74, +16 pp expected
  • Time, cost, quality and lessons learned, then the approval workflow

Workforce decision: L&D + Agility

HR Director

  • Two dimensions selected: +12 pp expected, 67/100 modeled organizational position
  • L&D: 52 → 60, target 70, +8 pp
  • Agility: 58 → 74, target 74, +16 pp

Workforce decision: all six dimensions

HR Director

  • Agility +16, Efficiency +14, L&D +8, ESAT +6, D&I +0, ESG +0 pp
  • Equal-weight aggregate: 52 → 59.3, target 66.3
