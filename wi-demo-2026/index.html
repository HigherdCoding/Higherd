Workforce Intelligence · Use case 06

The project environment

Converter Delivery, an energy transmission project in construction and commissioning. Work package WP-EL-042 covers protection and control commissioning for an AC yard: relay configuration, protection testing, IED communications and SCADA interfaces.

Current team size
18
Specialists required
2 FTE
Required readiness
8 weeks

The challenge

The work package needs 2 FTE of protection engineering within 8 weeks, but the team has no confirmed internal specialist.

  • Protection system engineering: 0 of 2 FTE available
  • Relay design, grid interfaces and commissioning evidence: 1 of 2 FTE each
  • Agility and efficiency are below agreed team targets
  • Options to review: internal mobility, external candidates or temporary specialist contracting

The role: Protection & Control Commissioning Engineer

A contract engagement, onsite in Germany, with 3+ years in P&C commissioning. The requirement separates general engineering skills from task-related ones and lists the test equipment the project uses.
